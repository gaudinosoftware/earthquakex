Privacy Policy

earthquakeX for iPhone, formerly earthquakePR. Effective October 17, 2026.

The short version

  • There’s no earthquakeX account. We never ask for your name or email.
  • Your location stays on your iPhone. It’s never sent to us.
  • Your I’m Safe Contacts stay on your iPhone. Messages go only to the people you choose, and only when you send them.
  • To send alerts, our server knows your alert settings and your region, linked to a random ID, not to you.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell data.

Earthquake data

earthquakeX downloads earthquake information from the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) and the European-Mediterranean Seismological Centre (EMSC), and tsunami messages from our alerts server. These are requests for public data. They don’t include your location or anything about you, other than your device’s IP address, as with any internet connection.

Location

If you allow it, earthquakeX uses your location while you’re using the app to show you on the map, to show how far earthquakes are from you, to suggest your region, and to know whether a strong earthquake was near you. This happens on your iPhone. Your location is never sent to us or to the alert service.

To suggest a region, and when you search for a place, earthquakeX uses Apple’s map services to look up place names, the same way the Maps app does. Apple’s privacy policy applies to those lookups.

earthquakeX never uses your location in the background. You can change location access anytime in the Settings app, under earthquakeX.

Alerts

Alerts are sent with OneSignal, a notification service. When you turn on alerts:

  • OneSignal receives a random ID for this install, your iPhone’s push token, and basic device information such as the app version, iOS version, device model, language and time zone, plus your IP address, as with any internet connection.
  • Our alerts server receives that same random ID with your alert settings: your region, your minimum magnitude, your language, and whether you want major earthquakes worldwide and the aftershock Live Activity.

Neither receives your name, email, location or contacts. This information is used only to send you the alerts you asked for. When you turn off alerts, your settings are removed from our server.

I’m Safe and I Need Help

The contacts you add to I’m Safe Contacts are chosen with Apple’s contact picker, which only gives earthquakeX the people you pick, not your whole address book. They’re stored on your iPhone.

I’m Safe and I Need Help write a message in the Messages app, addressed to those contacts. Nothing is sent until you tap Send. I Need Help includes your exact location in the message, and asks for permission to use it first. These messages go from your iPhone to your contacts. We never receive them.

Seismic reports

Reports in the Activity tab are written on your iPhone, with Apple Intelligence’s on-device model when it’s available. Nothing is sent to Apple’s servers or to us.

Photos

earthquakeX only asks to add to your photo library when you choose to save an earthquake image. It never reads your photos.

Sharing

When you share an earthquake, it goes only where you choose to send it. We don’t receive a copy.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t collect your name, email, location or contacts.
  • We don’t use advertising or analytics services. The only third-party service is OneSignal, for alerts, as described above.
  • We don’t track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don’t sell or share personal information.

If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. You can change this in Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

  • To stop alerts and remove your alert settings from our server, turn off Earthquake Alerts in the Alerts tab.
  • To remove a contact, go to Settings › I’m Safe Contacts and swipe it away.
  • Deleting the app removes everything earthquakeX stored on that device.

Children

earthquakeX isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top.
